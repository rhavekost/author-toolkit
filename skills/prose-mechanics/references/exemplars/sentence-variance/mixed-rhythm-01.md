**Before (five 5-word sentences in a row):** The dog ran fast. The cat watched from high. The bird flew away quick. The wind blew through leaves. The sun set slow tonight.
**After:** The dog ran fast. From the fence, the cat watched, still as stone, waiting. The bird flew. Wind moved through the leaves in long, unhurried waves as the sun went down.
Breaking the uniform length lets some sentences breathe and others land short and hard.
