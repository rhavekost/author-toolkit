# Clichés Audit Reference

List-based detection of well-worn phrases (curated list mirrored here for
the no-engine path), judged in-session for whether the usage is genuinely
tired or deliberately, ironically, or idiomatically fine.

## What This Catches
Exact-match stock phrases from a curated list of 26 common clichés:

- "at the end of the day"
- "in the nick of time"
- "cold as ice"
- "quiet as a mouse"
- "dead as a doornail"
- "avoid like the plague"
- "heart of gold"
- "only time will tell"
- "easier said than done"
- "the calm before the storm"
- "it was a dark and stormy night"
- "little did she know"
- "little did he know"
- "her blood ran cold"
- "his blood ran cold"
- "sent shivers down her spine"
- "sent shivers down his spine"
- "let out a breath she didn't know she was holding"
- "let out a breath he didn't know he was holding"
- "a mixture of emotions"
- "butterflies in her stomach"
- "butterflies in his stomach"
- "time stood still"
- "in the blink of an eye"
- "each and every"
- "few and far between"

## What This Does NOT Catch
Clichéd IDEAS expressed in fresh language (a stock plot beat described
without stock phrasing) — this audit is lexical, not structural.
Redundancies not on the list ("reversed back") are left to session
judgment.

## Judgment Protocol
Run `scriptorium prose prepare cliches <chapter>`, judge each candidate,
then `submit-findings`. Without the engine, scan for the listed phrases
plus any other tired constructions by eye.
